# 🌍 Water Scarcity EDA Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
Water scarcity is becoming one of the most critical global challenges, driven by increasing population, rising demand, and unsustainable groundwater usage.

This project performs an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to uncover hidden patterns, relationships, and key drivers behind water scarcity using real-world inspired data.

The goal is to:
- Understand **what causes water scarcity**
- Identify **high-risk regions and trends**
- Provide **data-driven insights for sustainability**

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
Despite sufficient rainfall in many regions, water scarcity continues to rise.

This project answers:
- Is rainfall really the main factor behind scarcity?
- How does **groundwater depletion** affect availability?
- What role does **population growth** play?
- Are we using water **sustainably or over-extracting**?

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset contains region-wise and year-wise water-related indicators.

### 🔑 Features:
- **Region / State / District** → Location identifiers
- **Year** → Time-based analysis
- **Rainfall (mm)** → Annual rainfall
- **Groundwater Level (m)** → Depth of groundwater
- **Water Extraction (MCM)** → Water usage
- **Water Recharge (MCM)** → Natural replenishment
- **Population** → Total population
- **Water Demand (MLD)** → Required water
- **Water Supply (MLD)** → Available water
- **Scarcity Index** → Overall water stress indicator

### ⚙️ Engineered Features:
- Water Deficit  
- Groundwater Stress  
- Extraction–Recharge Ratio

## 📈 Key Insights
- 🚨 Water scarcity is mainly caused by **demand–supply imbalance**, not just rainfall  
- 📉 Groundwater levels show a **continuous decline** → over-extraction  
- ⚠️ In many regions, **extraction > recharge** → unsustainable usage  
- 👥 Population growth directly increases **water demand pressure**  
- 🌧️ High rainfall ≠ low scarcity (inefficient storage & recharge)  
- 📍 Certain regions show **persistent high scarcity** → structural issues

## 💡 Recommendations
- Implement **groundwater extraction control policies**  
- Promote **rainwater harvesting & recharge systems**  
- Focus on **demand-side management** in high population areas  
- Improve **water storage & distribution systems**  
- Use **data analytics for continuous monitoring**  

---

## 🧠 Conclusion
Water scarcity is not just a natural issue — it is largely **human-driven**.

This analysis clearly shows:
- Overuse of groundwater  
- Poor demand management  
- Lack of efficient recharge systems  

👉 Data-driven decisions are essential for **sustainable water management**
